Bristol Lean Users and Enthusiasts

The BLUE group aims to bring together people interested in Lean and formalisation in the local area surrounding Bristol (UK). We meet every other month in the Fry Building in Bristol.

To hear about meetings and events, join our mailing list BLUE@JISCMAIL.AC.UK. You can subscribe or unsubscribe at any time. Feel free to contact any member for other requests.
